EMERGING DIRECTION

Agentic AI — Scientific Workflows

Connecting AI agents with scientific software, computational modelling, and human oversight.

I am interested in building agentic AI systems for scientific research that combine large language models, machine-learning models, molecular modelling tools, and reproducible computation. My aim is to develop workflows that can plan analysis steps, call scientific software, assess outputs, and involve researchers in decisions that need scientific judgement.

Areas I want to develop

  • LLM-based agents and multi-agent coordination for scientific tasks
  • Agent-controlled cheminformatics and QSAR screening workflows
  • Tool-assisted protein and antibody-design workflows
  • Automated molecular dynamics setup, analysis and reporting
  • Human-in-the-loop validation, traceability and reproducibility

Current direction: This is a developing research and engineering interest, not a claim that all these systems have already been implemented.

RESEARCH PORTFOLIO

Scientific Projects

My peer-reviewed publications represent my main scientific research projects. These short descriptions are placeholders for detailed case studies written in my own words.

ML-Assisted Antibiotic Designing and antibiotic permeability

Exploratory work: fine-tuning a pretrained S4 model using antibiotic- and permeability-related datasets, with an interest in beta-lactam antibiotic design. Publication: Fast prediction of antibiotic permeability through membrane channels using Brownian dynamics. Biophysical Journal (2023).

Focus: Machine-learning-based antibiotic design (exploratory work) and mechanistic modelling of antibiotic transport and permeability (published research).

Vitamin B12 transporter protein complexes

Publication: BtuB TonB-dependent transporters and BtuG surface lipoproteins form stable complexes for vitamin B12 uptake in gut Bacteroides. Nature Communications (2023).

Focus: Protein complex formation and membrane transport.

Mirror-image peptide nanopores

Publications: Assembly of transmembrane pores from mirror-image peptides. Nature Communications (2022); Fabrication of cytotoxic mirror image nanopores. Nature Communications (2025).

Focus: Peptide nanostructures and structure–function relationships.

Alpha-helical peptide nanopores for biomarker profiling

Publication: A self-assembled peptide forms α-helical nanopores for ultrasensitive biomarker profiling. Nature Nanotechnology.

Focus: Peptide self-assembly and biomarker profiling.

Investigation of Magnetic Field Effect via Classical Mechanics

Report: Molekulardynamik von Proteinfaltung unter Einfluss von externen magnetischen Feldern.

Focus: Influence of applied the static magnetic field in classical molecular dynamics simulation on transport properties of ions, SPC/E and TIP3P water models as wells as protein folding under the influence of external magnetic fields. Development magnetic field simulation code for BRODEA, and OpenMM as well as integration into GROMACS and NAMD code.
